Scenario & assumptions

Haven & Grain wants a readable review of four fictional Sponsored Products campaigns. The planning target is 25% ACOS, selected only for this exercise. Product margins, organic sales, attribution delays, and fees are unknown.

No account changes were made. Ad sales are attributed revenue, not profit. This snapshot does not prove a trend or an improvement.

What the mock data suggests

  1. Branded traffic is more efficient in this snapshot. Its 10.9% ACOS is below the exercise target, but branded and discovery traffic serve different purposes. Confirm brand demand and profitability before moving budget.
  2. Non-brand exact merits a closer review. Its 18.5% ACOS and 36 orders make it a candidate for checking budget limits, individual targets, and placement performance.
  3. Auto and broad need a search term review. Their ACOS is above the exercise target. Review relevance and converting terms before making blanket cuts that could reduce useful discovery.

Recommended next steps

  1. Check attribution completeness, product margins, stock availability, and the seller’s actual ACOS target.
  2. Review mismatched search terms for possible negative targeting, with seller approval.
  3. Test relevant converting discovery terms in a separate exact campaign; avoid uncontrolled overlap.
  4. Make small, documented bid or budget changes only after reviewing target and placement data.
  5. Compare comparable reporting periods and record what changed. Avoid claiming an improvement from a single snapshot.

Calculation checks

ACOS = ad spend ÷ attributed ad sales × 100

ROAS = attributed ad sales ÷ ad spend

CPC = spend ÷ clicks = $920 ÷ 870 = $1.06

CTR = clicks ÷ impressions × 100 = 0.81%

Order conversion rate = orders ÷ clicks × 100 = 12.64%

Reference: Amazon Ads: understanding ACOS.
