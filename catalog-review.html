02 / The important decision

A missing field in the feed is not a confirmed error.

PUBLIC FEED96 images

Image records with no alt text value in the public product feed.

PRODUCT PAGE SPOT CHECKAlt text present

Two product pages checked by hand both had image alt text in their HTML.

Decision: report this as "confirm whether the field is available," not "fix 96 accessibility errors." The public feed can leave out fields that the live store page still shows. Confirming with the store's admin records comes before any change.

04 / How the data was collected

An adapted n8n workflow, credited to its author.

The workflow started from the n8n library template #18502 by Daniel Shashko. Its two product parser steps were kept unchanged. I replaced the paid proxy and Gmail steps with direct public requests, added variant-level organization and field checks, and saved the result as a JSON report. No AI step, emails, or store changes were involved.

Review rules

Keep titles, options, and SKUs exactly as returned. Flag missing or repeated SKUs. Keep each store's currency instead of guessing from the "$" sign.

Scope

A first-page sample of 12 products per store, not a full store audit. No sales, orders, costs, or private inventory data.
