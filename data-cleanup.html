03 / The rules I followed

Fix what is clear. Ask about what is not.

Standardize

Trim extra spaces. Use proper capitals for names and companies. Lowercase emails. One phone format (+63 917 555 0101) and one date format (YYYY-MM-DD).

De-duplicate

Two rows with the same email are the same contact. Keep the first, remove the repeat, and record it in the change log.

Never guess

A missing email or an incomplete address like "ana.cruz@example" is flagged, not invented. The client decides what the right value is.

04 / The handoff

What the client receives.

The clean file, the untouched original, and a change log that lists every edited field, removed duplicate, and flagged contact with a reason. Nothing is deleted without a record, so any change can be checked or undone.
