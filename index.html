FAQ

Questions clients often ask.

Don't see yours? Ask me on a free call or by email.

What can fit in the $18 starter task?

One clearly defined task that takes about 3 hours. For example: cleaning up a spreadsheet, preparing a few product listings, or sorting and labeling an inbox. We agree on the scope before I start, so you know exactly what you'll get.

Can I start small and add more hours later?

Yes. Start with the starter task or a 10-hour block ($60). When you're ready, move to part-time (20 hours/week) or full-time (40 hours/week).

What access do you need to my accounts?

Only what the task needs, and you decide what to share. Shared folders, delegated inbox access, or a team login you control are usually enough. Access is agreed before work starts.

Which time zones can you work in?

I have a flexible schedule and can work US, UK, or AU hours, including graveyard shifts. We set the working hours together before we start.

Do you use AI tools?

Yes. I use tools like ChatGPT and Claude to draft and organize faster. I review everything before it reaches you, and I only use AI tools with your files if you're comfortable with it.
