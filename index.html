A FEW USEFUL DETAILS

Before we start.

Are these paid client projects?+

No. They’re independent demos using a fictional store and sample records. They show how I approach the work; they do not claim client results.

Can you work with my store’s guidelines?+

Yes. I can work from your SOPs, templates, and product source information. I flag unclear details and requests that need an owner’s decision.

What does basic automation support cover?+

Simple workflow planning, field mapping, and review steps. My demo shows a proposed request-to-draft process. It is a simulation, with no connected apps or live AI service.

What’s a good first project?+

A small catalog cleanup, a set of customer reply templates, or an order tracker. We can agree on a manageable scope and review the result together.
