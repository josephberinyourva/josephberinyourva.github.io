01 / THE EDIT

Lead with the shape. Keep care easy to find.

Identify it

The title retains the brand and corner-spoon product type, then adds material and length.

Explain the use

The opening connects the straight-sided shape to cookware corners. It avoids vague “premium quality” language.

Answer care questions

Hand washing is explicit. No dishwasher suitability or heat rating is added.

02 / COPY DECISIONS

What the rewrite earns its space with.

FieldDecisionReason
Product typeKeep “corner spoon”Preserves the feature that distinguishes this SKU.

A draft with a clear next step.

Confirm the seller’s intended market, current packaging, image permission, price, and required fields. Review the rendered listing after an authorized upload. This project delivers a researched copy proposal; no changes were made to OXO or Amazon.
