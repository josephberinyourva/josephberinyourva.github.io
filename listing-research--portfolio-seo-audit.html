07 / What to measure after implementation

  1. Use Search Console, with owner access, to confirm coverage and inspect the intended canonical URLs.
  2. Record query and page impressions, clicks, CTR and position for consistent date ranges. Separate branded and service-related queries.
  3. Track completed inquiries separately from contact-button clicks. Opening an email application is not a confirmed lead.
  4. Log publication dates and other changes. Avoid claiming that a ranking or lead change was caused by one copy edit without stronger evidence.

Not assessed: backlinks, search demand, crawl logs, Core Web Vitals, server response headers, exhaustive link health or actual Google indexation. No search-performance baseline or improvement has been invented.
