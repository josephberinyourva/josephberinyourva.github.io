03 / THE IMPORTANT FINDING

A matching name is not enough.

INDIVIDUAL PRODUCT · SKU 105802112 × 3 × 1 in

Slotted spoon dimensions on its own product page.

SET · SKU 125608014 × 3 × 1 in

Slotted spoon component dimensions on the set page.

Decision: retain each source value on its own record. Do not merge the two or “correct” one from the other. Ask whether the set contains a different size or whether a page needs updating; the public pages alone do not settle that question.

This is the kind of discrepancy to resolve before assigning variants or publishing a combined product page.

04 / HANDOFF NOTES

What stays out of the file.

Seller price & stock

No store inventory or pricing sheet was supplied. Manufacturer retail prices are not a seller’s cost or stock record.

Marketplace variants

These are separate product records. A shared brand and material do not establish a valid parent–child listing.

Claims & assets

Check image rights and current packaging before a commercial listing. No certifications, heat limits, or country-of-origin claims were inferred.
